# What FixedItNow is

FixedItNow is a Bangladesh-focused marketplace that connects customers with local service professionals for home repairs, maintenance and everyday services such as electrical work, plumbing, AC servicing, cleaning, appliance repair, painting, carpentry, moving, pest control and computer support.

# What "profile reviewed" means

A professional appears in search only after they finished onboarding, a FixedItNow reviewer approved the profile, and the professional chose to publish it. Profiles that are suspended by an administrator are hidden again.
Approval of a profile is a review by FixedItNow staff. It is not a government background check unless the platform says otherwise.

# What the platform does not show yet

Ratings are shown only from reviews left by customers whose booking was completed and paid (the payment verified). A professional with no such reviews shows no rating. Distance or map search is not available: location is matched by division (for example Dhaka or Sylhet) and the professional's listed service areas.
