# Opening a dispute

Either the customer or the professional can open one dispute per booking when there is a problem such as service quality, a no-show, a price disagreement or conduct. A dispute can be opened when a job is in progress, waiting for completion confirmation, completed within the last 14 days, or confirmed and past its start time. Describe the problem in writing (20 to 2000 characters). Uploading evidence files is not supported yet.

# What happens next

A FixedItNow administrator reviews the dispute, may ask for more information, and then resolves it: mark the booking completed, cancel it, or restore it. The opener can withdraw the dispute. There is no automatic refund or payout outcome; a refund is a separate request.
