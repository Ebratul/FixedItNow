# How professionals price their work

Each professional lists the services they offer with a pricing model: a fixed price, an hourly rate, or by quote. The starting price on search cards is the lowest priced service of that professional. It is a starting point, not a final price. Professionals who work by quote only show "price on request".

# Quotes

For quote-priced jobs the professional sends a quote with labour, materials and other costs. The total is calculated by the platform from those parts. A quote can be revised by the professional, and only one quote is pending at a time. You can accept or reject a quote. A quote expires after its validity period. Accepting a quote confirms the booking at exactly the quoted total.

# Agreed price

When a booking is confirmed, the agreed price is stored and used for payment. The platform adds no fee or commission to the agreed price.
