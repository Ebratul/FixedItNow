# Cancelling

A customer can cancel a booking before it is confirmed (a reason is optional) and after it is confirmed (a reason of at least 5 characters is required). A professional can cancel only a confirmed booking, with a reason. Before confirmation a professional uses "decline" instead.
Nobody can cancel once the job has started or after it is completed. In that situation use a dispute. Cancelling frees the time slot and closes any open quotes or reschedule requests.

# Fees and refunds on cancellation

The platform has not defined cancellation fees, penalties, percentages or refund deadlines. Cancelling does not refund money automatically. If you already paid for a cancelled booking you can request a refund, and a FixedItNow administrator reviews it. No refund amount or outcome is promised in advance.

# Rescheduling

Either side can ask to move a confirmed booking to a new time of the same length. The other side accepts or rejects. The original appointment stays valid until the request is accepted. If the new time is taken in the meantime, nothing changes. A reschedule request expires after 48 hours or at the appointment start, whichever comes first.
