# How to join

Create an account, verify your email, and complete the worker profile steps: display name, headline, bio, experience, languages, service areas and the services you offer with prices. Then submit the profile for review.

# Review and publishing

A FixedItNow reviewer approves the profile, asks for changes, or declines it. After approval you decide when to publish the profile. Only approved and published profiles appear in search. You can unpublish at any time. An administrator can suspend a profile, which hides it.
Identity-document verification is not available yet.

# Availability

To receive booking requests you must publish weekly working hours. You can also add time off. Changing your hours never cancels confirmed bookings.
