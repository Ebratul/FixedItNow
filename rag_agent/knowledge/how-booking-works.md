# Requesting a booking

Open a professional's public profile and choose "Request a booking". You pick a service, a day and a time slot, and describe the job and your address. Only the bookable start times that the professional has published are offered. If a professional has published no working hours, they cannot be booked yet and their profile says so.

# Time rules

All times are in Bangladesh time. A request must start at least 2 hours from now and no more than 90 days ahead. Appointments start and end on 30-minute marks and last between 30 minutes and 8 hours.

# What happens after you request

The request is sent to the professional. If the professional's price is fixed or hourly, they accept and the booking is confirmed at that price. If their price is by quote, they send you a quote and the booking is confirmed when you accept it. The professional can also decline. A request that nobody answers before its start time expires.
A professional's availability is confirmed only through this booking flow. Search results do not guarantee that a professional is free at a given time.

# Progress of a job

A confirmed booking moves to in progress when the professional starts the job, then waits for your confirmation after the professional marks the work complete. Only you, the customer, can confirm completion.

# Who sees what

Only you and the assigned professional can see a booking. The professional sees your street address only after the booking is confirmed; before that they see just your area.
