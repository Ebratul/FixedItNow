# Paying for a booking

Payment is not required to confirm a booking. After a booking is confirmed, you can pay the agreed price from the booking page using a payment method the platform currently offers. The checkout page shows which methods are available, and it shows a method as unavailable when it is not configured. bKash and SSLCOMMERZ are the supported providers when enabled.
You pay the full agreed price. Deposits and instalments are not supported.

# When a payment counts as paid

A payment is recorded as successful only after FixedItNow has verified it directly with the payment provider. A redirect back to the site or a message on screen alone is not proof of payment. If the result is not known yet, the payment page shows it as processing and checks again.

# Worker payouts

FixedItNow does not hold money in escrow and does not pay professionals through the platform yet. "Payment verified" means the platform's payment provider account received your payment.

# Refunds

You can request a refund for a verified payment only when the booking was cancelled. A request does not move money. An administrator reviews it, and a refund is shown as completed only after the payment provider confirms it. The platform has not defined refund percentages or time limits, so each request is decided by a person.
